# Reading a Bug Bounty Scope

The scope defines what you are *allowed* to test. Testing out of scope can get
you banned — or worse, legally exposed.

## What to look for

- **In-scope assets** — domains, apps, and APIs you may target.
- **Out-of-scope assets** — explicitly forbidden targets.
- **Accepted vulnerability classes** — what the program pays for.
- **Rules of engagement** — rate limits, account creation, automation policy.

Always re-read scope before you start, and again before you submit.
